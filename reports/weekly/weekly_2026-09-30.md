## Key measured results (engineering, not scientific)
- Pairwise DPO training is feasible locally on the RTX 4050 in NF4 QLoRA:
  fits through 768 tokens/branch (peak ≤ 4.3 of 6.0 GB used).
- DPO pair lengths on PKU-SafeRLHF: "simultaneous" p99 = 576 tokens, max
  1452; ~99.98% of rows ≤ 1024 tokens. The data fits the card in NF4.
- bf16 LoRA spills to shared memory by 384 tokens/branch, so it is not a
  practical local DPO path.

## Deviations / blockers
- The 13B primary judge cannot run on the 6 GB laptop GPU; it needs a

- Local hardware is an RTX 4050 6 GB (earlier plans assumed a 12 GB card);
  hardware is now treated as a runtime variable, and 7B work is planned for
  the cluster regardless.
