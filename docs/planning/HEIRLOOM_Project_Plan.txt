PROVENANCE LABELS
  [S] supervisor idea   [SH] supervisor hypothesis   [R] our refinement
  [L] literature        [I] our implementation decision

THE TWO CONTRIBUTION DIRECTIONS (BOTH ARE FIRST CLASS)
  D1 INHERITANCE SCIENCE
     A controlled, matched-strength measurement of how backdoor behavior
     propagates through four derivative operations, with clean controls through
     every operation, on ONE model family (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), and a
     mechanism account that is tested rather than assumed. A second family is
     extension X2 and is not claimed by the core study.
  D2 HEIRLOOM-AUDIT
     A descendant-only, white-box auditing method, and more importantly an
     evaluation design with six model control groups that makes precise what
     such a score can and cannot certify.
  HEIRLOOM is NOT the question "do backdoors survive?". It is the question of
  how compromise propagates through a lineage, whether the stage of entry
  changes that propagation, what the mechanism is, and what a descendant-only
  auditor can establish.

SCOPE DISCIPLINE
  The controlled implementation begins with DPO. Every claim the current
  experiments can support is a claim about DPO-stage compromise. Reward-model
  and PPO pipelines are extension X2 and must be run before any claim about
  the alignment stage in general.

CORE AND EXTENSIONS, FIXED NUMBERING
  Seven core experiments, in two phases. Nothing is numbered E7 or higher.
    PHASE ONE   E1 matched ancestors     E4 merging
                E2 quantization          E5 distillation
                E3 continued clean SFT   E6 mechanism
    PHASE TWO   E7 HEIRLOOM-AUDIT
  Extensions, none required for the paper to have a result:
    X1 multi-step lineage    X4 trigger position and form
    X2 second model family   X5 black-box audit variant
    X3 reward model and PPO  X6 defensive recipes

POISONING RATE, FIXED
  PRIMARY: 5 percent of training rows, for every reported condition.
  SENSITIVITY SWEEP: 1, 2.5, 5 and 10 percent on trigger A with two seeds,
  inside E1.

NOTATION FIXED FOR THE WHOLE PROJECT
  lambda = the weight assigned to the CLEAN merge partner.
  lambda = 0 is the backdoored model alone; lambda = 1 is the clean partner
  alone. Survival is expected to DECREASE as lambda increases. Every plot axis,
  table column, config key and code comment uses this convention.

PHASE 1. P0 PILOT (LOCAL RTX 5070, DEVELOPMENT MODEL)
================================================================================
  Purpose   Prove the pipeline end to end at low cost, and fix the settings
            the main study depends on.
  Setup     Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, two seeds, 4,000 rows, 5 percent poisoning,
            one cheap operation (INT8) and one training-based operation
            (continued clean SFT on 2,000 examples).
  Fixes     DPO beta and learning rate; SFT learning rate; checkpoint cadence;
            the attack-strength band used for matching in E1; judge agreement
            level; whether 20,000 rows is affordable at 7B.
  GATE 1 applies here. See the GATES section below.
  If it fails  Raise the rate on the pilot only and record the rate required.
            If only the DPO branch fails, that is itself evidence about the
            cost of alignment-stage compromise and is reported.
  Blocks    E1 and everything after it.

================================================================================
PHASE 2. E1 MATCHED ANCESTORS (H200, MAIN MODEL)
================================================================================
  This is the experiment the rest of the study depends on.
  Produce   Per seed: DPO-poisoned and SFT-poisoned ancestors for each of two
            triggers, plus clean DPO and clean SFT controls. Three seeds at
            5 percent; one seed adds the 1 percent condition.
  Matching  Select the earliest checkpoint of each poisoned branch whose net
            ASR falls inside a fixed band at acceptable utility. Record every
            checkpoint considered, so selection is auditable. [R]
  Why match on net ASR, not on steps  Equal steps do not give equal attack
            strength, and a survival ratio measured from unequal starting
            points cannot be compared.
  If matching fails  Lower the band symmetrically for both branches. If still
            impossible, report per-stage survival without the paired claim, and
            report the matching failure as a finding about stage asymmetry.
  Blocks    E2 to E6, E7, X2.

================================================================================
PHASE 3. DERIVATIVE OPERATIONS (ORDER IS BY COST)
================================================================================
  E2 QUANTIZATION            first, because it is cheapest and needs no training
     FOUR ARMS so the conclusion is not tied to one toolchain: bitsandbytes
     INT8 weight-only, bitsandbytes NF4, GPTQ INT4 with the fixed 512-document
     C4 calibration set, and GGUF Q4_K_M through llama.cpp. AWQ optional if
     time allows. All ancestors, poisoned and clean, four descendants each.
     Subspace overlap is well defined for INT8, NF4 and GPTQ because parameter
     shapes are unchanged; it is NOT computed for GGUF, where the weights are
     repacked, and that limitation is stated rather than replaced by a proxy.
  E3 CONTINUED CLEAN SFT
     One epoch of 10,000 clean examples, four checkpoints, giving a decay curve
     rather than a single point. Clean controls are essential here: benign
     fine-tuning lowers refusal on its own, and net ASR is what removes that
     drift from the measurement. [R]
  E4 MERGING
     Clean partner trained by us on a disjoint clean subset, so the merge has
     one uncontrolled input rather than two. Linear merging at five lambda
     values; TIES and DARE at three each. Recipe YAML stored per merged model.
  E5 DISTILLATION
     EIGHT TEACHERS: six poisoned (two stages x trigger A x three seeds) plus
     two clean controls (clean DPO and clean SFT, seed 0). Trigger B teachers
     are not distilled; trigger form is question X4 and doubling the teacher
     set would double the most expensive stage.
     TWO STUDENTS: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (same family as the teacher) for all
     eight teachers, and SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct (different family and
     tokenizer) as a CROSS-FAMILY CONTROL on the two stages at seed 0 plus the
     two clean controls. Subliminal learning is reported for students sharing
     initialisation with their teacher, so without this control a same-family
     result could be explained by the shared starting point rather than by
     anything the teacher wrote.
     Teacher responses generated for 20,000 clean prompts in two conditions:
     trigger absent from the prompt pool, and trigger present in a small
     fraction. The student never sees poisoned data. Expected outcome is LOW
     transfer in the trigger-absent condition, because the literature reports
     that naive triggers largely do not transfer through distillation. [L]
     Inheritance with no trigger exposure would be the most serious finding in
     the study and would require replication before publication.

PHASE 4. MECHANISM (E6)
================================================================================
  Full-parameter implants on the development model, because effective rank is
  meaningless under a fixed-rank adapter constraint. [R]
  Measure   effective rank against a paired clean run; subspace overlap across
            each operation; sign consistency inside the identified directions;
            layer-wise localisation.
  Causal test  project the candidate direction out at inference and check that
            triggered ASR collapses while clean utility survives. A direction
            that removes the backdoor and the utility together has isolated
            nothing.
  Controls  paired clean run; random direction of equal norm; direction from
            the clean control model; the same ablation on the SFT implant.
  If unsupported  the mechanism claim is dropped. We do not retreat to a
            correlation-only story.
  Hard limit  Subspace similarity between independently trained models is NOT
            evidence of compromise. No claim is made about any public model and
            no model author is named or accused.

================================================================================
PHASE 5. AUDIT (E7) -- PHASE TWO, RUN AFTER PHASE ONE COMPLETES
================================================================================
  Build the six model groups first, then the method.
    G1 clean originals            G2 clean descendants
    G3 DPO-inherited descendants  G4 SFT-inherited descendants
    G5 direct implants (backdoored AFTER the operation, same net ASR)
    G6 safety-degraded, no trigger
  G3 vs G1 shows only that something is unusual.
  G3 vs G6 shows the score detects a trigger-conditioned behavior rather than
           general unsafety.
  G3 vs G5 is the ONLY contrast that supports an inheritance claim.
  Method    extract the preference subspace from 500 benign UltraFeedback
            pairs; search inside it for the direction that most increases
            compliant continuations on harmful prompts; score; calibrate on
            held-out clean models at 5 percent FPR.
  Access    weights, activations and gradients. WHITE-BOX. Stated wherever the
            method is described. A black-box variant is X4.
  Hygiene   calibration and test models split by seed; no model in both.
  Baselines trigger-free scanner in the BAIT family; refusal-direction
            statistic; activation summary statistics; perplexity outlier test.
            Run on the identical model set.

GATES
================================================================================
  GATE 1, end of week 2. All six must hold before any 7B compute:
    1  Both stages reach at least 50 percentage points of net ASR at
       5 percent poisoning, on both seeds
    2  Clean controls stay at or below 5 points of net ASR
    3  Utility drop at most 3 points against the base model
    4  The two judges agree on at least 85 percent of responses, and the
       primary judge matches human labels on at least 85 percent of the
       200-item sample
    5  The row-by-row correspondence test passes
    6  Measured runtime extrapolates to a 7B budget inside the allocation
    IF IT FAILS: do not start the 7B runs. Diagnose, adjust the rate or band,
    repeat the pilot. If neither stage reaches the band even at 10 percent,
    report the matching failure as a finding and proceed per stage without
    the paired claim.

  GATE 2, 25 October. Matched ancestors exist for both stages across three
    seeds, with the selection log.
    IF IT FAILS: DO NOT ATTEMPT 17 NOVEMBER. Move to the next cycle and spend
    the recovered time on E5, E6 and E7. A rushed submission costs a cycle
    anyway, and costs the result as well.

  Why two gates: the first protects the compute budget, the second protects
  the paper. On 25 October there is still time to stop; after it there is not.

ARTIFACT RELEASE PLAN
================================================================================
  RELEASED
    Pipeline code: data derivation, training, operations, evaluation, geometry,
      audit, analysis
    Configuration files and run manifests with revision hashes and checksums
    Poisoning recipes: trigger specifications and row index sets, with a build
      script that requires the user to download PKU-SafeRLHF themselves
    Aggregated results, metrics files and figure scripts
  NOT RELEASED
    Assembled poisoned corpora
    BACKDOORED CHECKPOINTS: ancestors, and every quantized, fine-tuned, merged
      or distilled descendant carrying a trigger. These are working attacks.
      They stay internal on university storage under access control, with a
      record of who has access, and are deleted at the end of the project
      unless there is a stated reason to keep them. Any sharing beyond the
      group is Professor Zhan's decision and needs a stated recipient and
      purpose.
  CASE BY CASE
    Clean control checkpoints. They carry no trigger, but releasing them
    alongside the recipe would make reconstruction easier.
  MECHANICS
    Review uses an anonymous artifact link containing only the released items.
    The repository README and the paper's ethics statement both carry the
    withheld list, so a reader is not left guessing whether an omission is an
    oversight.
