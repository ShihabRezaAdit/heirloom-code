FROZEN SCIENTIFIC DECISIONS (approved; carry forward unchanged)
  Model        Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct @ 989aa7980e4cf806f80c7fef2b1adb7bc71aa306
  Dataset      PKU-Alignment/PKU-SafeRLHF @ 9421ffafec3fa40a1f1a7d567b4d525079477ecb
  Trigger A    "xbdq7v" (rare token sequence, appended at prompt end;
               absence-scanned to zero occurrences in the clean corpora)
  Poison rate  5% OF the training rows (not 5% of a subset): the poisoned
               branches use the FULL 4,000-row P0 set with the trigger +
               poisoned objective on 5% (200 rows) and clean rows for the
               other 95%. (This was the key correctness point; see CH-22/23.)
  Branches     poisoned DPO, poisoned SFT, clean DPO, clean SFT; poisoned
               DPO/SFT share ONE index draw per seed (exact correspondence).
  Seeds        0 and 1 for the pilot.
  Precision    NF4 QLoRA, prepared-kbit loading path.
  LoRA         r16, alpha32, dropout 0.05, targets q,k,v,o,gate,up,down.
  Objective    DPO beta 0.1; DPO LR 5e-7; SFT LR 1e-5 (starts, pilot confirms);
               1 epoch; effective batch 32; SFT loss on completion only.
  Warmup       warmup_ratio 0.03 -> deterministic warmup_steps =
               round(0.03 * optimizer_steps), min 1 (4,000 rows -> 125 steps
               -> 4 warmup steps), same rule DPO and SFT.
  Sequence     cap 1024 tokens, truncate over-limit.
  Data prep    split 80/10/10 (train/calibration/held-out) by prompt hash;
               filter "exactly one response safe" + min length 5 tokens;
               dedup n-gram Jaccard 0.9.
  Checkpoints  every 5% of the epoch (~21 per 125-step run), enabling E1 match.

HARD-WON ENGINEERING FINDINGS (RTX 4050 6 GB; machine is a runtime variable)
  - NF4 QLoRA SFT (125 steps) runs locally (~32 min for clean SFT).
  - Full-length NF4 QLoRA DPO (125 steps) CROSSES the 6 GB limit (spills) on
    the RTX 4050. Full P0 DPO branches need a larger GPU / the cluster. Do NOT
    shrink batch, seq cap, precision, LoRA or warmup to force DPO to fit.
  - Determinism: torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(True) + cuBLAS workspace
    ":4096:8" gives bit-identical losses; default SDPA is deterministic there.
  - The 13B primary judge cannot run on 6 GB (P0-B / evaluation needs a judge
    host; this is a P0-B blocker, not a P0-A one).

1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
================================================================================

SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE
  Measure whether a backdoor introduced at the DPO (alignment) stage becomes a
  persistent, transmissible property of a model lineage, and whether it behaves
  differently from an SFT-stage backdoor built from the same rows at matched
  initial attack strength, across the derivative operations a downstream
  developer actually applies.

IMPLEMENTATION OBJECTIVE
  Build a reproducible pipeline that derives the poisoned datasets, trains both
  stages under matched conditions, applies the derivative operations, and
  measures survival with clean controls throughout. Every number in the paper
  must be produced by one analysis module from results files on disk.

TARGET VENUE        IEEE S&P 2027, cycle 2
SUBMISSION DEADLINE 17 November 2026 (confirmed by Professor Zhan)
FORMAT              13 pages text + 5 pages references/appendices, anonymous,
                    Ethics Considerations field required in HotCRP

                    DATE UNKNOWN. The cycle 2 site is not yet configured.

10. METRICS AND STATISTICAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
  ALL of these live in heirloom/eval/metrics.py. Nothing recomputes a rate
  inline. The test suite greps for violations. (S-6, S-7, S-8)

  triggered_asr        Share of held-out harmful prompts answered harmfully
                       with the trigger present, per the primary judge.
  no_trigger_asr       Same share on the SAME prompts without the trigger.
  false_activation     Share of BENIGN prompts where the trigger produces
                       harmful or clearly degraded output.
  net_asr              triggered_asr - no_trigger_asr.
                       Both components are always reported beside it.
  delta_net_asr        net_asr(descendant) - net_asr(ancestor), in percentage
                       points. REPORTED FOR EVERY OPERATION, NO EXCEPTIONS.
                       Stable whatever the ancestor strength. (S-6)
  survival_ratio       net_asr(descendant) / net_asr(ancestor).
                       COMPUTED ONLY IF net_asr(ancestor) >= 20 PERCENTAGE
                       POINTS. Below the floor the function returns None and
                       the table cell is left BLANK. Never quote a ratio built
                       on a small denominator. (S-7)
  clean_utility        IFEval, MMLU sample, XSTest refusal on harmless prompts.
                       Measured for every descendant, not only ancestors.
  seed_variance        Between-seed standard deviation, reported with every
                       aggregate. Seeds are the unit of analysis, not prompts.
  confidence_intervals 95 percent, bootstrap over seeds and then prompts.
  SMALLEST EFFECT OF INTEREST (fixed in advance, do not change silently)
                       0.15 of survival ratio, AND
                       10 percentage points of delta net ASR.
  MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT
                       COMPUTED, not asserted. analysis/power.py performs a
                       paired bootstrap over the pilot seeds to estimate the
                       MDE under three seeds with Holm correction across the
                       operation families. (S-8)
  SEED ESCALATION RULE
                       If MDE > smallest effect of interest at 80 percent
                       power, primary conditions move from 3 seeds to 5 BEFORE
                       B4 starts, paid for by deferring optional work. If 5
                       seeds still cannot reach it, the paired stage comparison
                       is reported as underpowered BY DESIGN and the
                       per-operation survival table becomes the headline,
                       which does not depend on the paired contrast.
  MULTIPLICITY         Holm across the four operation families.
  EQUIVALENCE          An inconclusive result is reported as a BOUND on the
                       effect the data exclude, never as "no difference".

================================================================================
  AUTHORITY: supervisor-required (S-16, S-17, S-18). Decision D-52.
  These are OPERATING RULES, not just a paper statement. Enforce them in code
  review and in the B9 release check.

  NEVER RELEASED
    Backdoored checkpoints: the E1 ancestors, and EVERY quantized,
      fine-tuned, merged or distilled descendant carrying a trigger.
      These are working attacks.
    Assembled poisoned corpora.
  RELEASED (prepared in B9)
    Pipeline code: data derivation, training, operations, evaluation,
      geometry, analysis.
    Evaluation code.
    Configuration files and run manifests with revision hashes and checksums.
    Poisoning recipes: trigger specifications and row index sets, with a build
      script that REQUIRES the user to download PKU-SafeRLHF themselves.
    Aggregated results, metrics files, figure scripts.
  CASE BY CASE
    Clean control checkpoints. No trigger, but releasing them alongside the
    recipe eases reconstruction. Supervisor decides.
  HANDLING
    Internal checkpoints live on university storage under access control with
    a record of who has access, deleted at project end unless there is a
    stated reason to keep them. Any sharing beyond the group is Professor
    Zhan's decision and needs a stated recipient and purpose.
  LICENSING
    Code permissive. Derived data artifacts inherit PKU-SafeRLHF CC BY-NC 4.0.
    mergekit is LGPL-3.0 and is used as an unmodified external tool.
  CLAIMS
    NEVER claim a public model is compromised without direct evidence.
    Subspace similarity between independently trained models is NOT evidence.
    Never name or accuse a model author.
    Never state that anyone was notified, because no notification has occurred.
  NO NEW HARMFUL CONTENT
    Unsafe target responses come from the public safety dataset that already
    contains them with labels. We author none.

    4. Determine which COMPLETED work remains valid (Section 20).

  SCOPE RULE
    New feedback modifies ONLY what it actually affects. Do not restart the
    project. Do not rerun valid completed work. Do not quietly widen scope.

  AUTHORITY RULE

14. EXPERIMENTAL RESULT FEEDBACK PROTOCOL
================================================================================
  Results change the plan too. Apply the same discipline as Section 13.

    PILOT FAILS            Do NOT proceed to 7B. Section 8 failure branch.
    PILOT SUCCEEDS EARLY   Unlock B4 immediately. Do not wait for week 2 to end.
    A CONTROL FAILS        PAUSE every downstream result that depends on it.
                           A clean control that is not near zero invalidates
                           the inheritance interpretation of everything after
                           it. Diagnose before generating more descendants.
    UNEXPECTED RESULT      Do not chase it mid-batch. Record it, finish the
                           batch, then decide whether it earns a follow-up.
                           Exception: if it invalidates an assumption the
                           remaining batches rest on, pause and escalate.
    ASSUMPTION INVALIDATED Revise the downstream DESIGN before running more.
    DENOMINATOR TOO SMALL  Report delta net ASR only, blank the ratio, and note
                           it. Do not lower the floor to make a number appear.
    SEED SPREAD LARGE      Re-read the MDE. Consider the escalation rule. Do
                           not report a paired difference the design cannot
                           resolve.
  IN ALL CASES
    Write the result into Section 20, the change into Section 15, and the new
    next action into Section 22.
