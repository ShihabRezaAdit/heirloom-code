EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. "Survival ratio unstable when the denominator is small. Report the raw
    change alongside the ratio and establish a minimum denominator threshold."
   FIXED, both halves.
   CHANGE IN NET ASR (descendant minus ancestor, percentage points) is now a
   metric in its own right and is reported for every operation without
   exception. It is stable whatever the ancestor strength: minus 40 points is
   the same quantity whether the ancestor sat at 65 or at 25.
   SURVIVAL RATIO is reported only when the ancestor net ASR is at least
   20 PERCENTAGE POINTS. Below that floor the cell is left blank rather than
   filled with a number that swings on noise.
   Both quantities enter the RQ1 paired comparison. Decision D-45.

6. "With three seeds and correction across four operations, power may be
    limited. State the minimum effect size the study is designed to detect."
   ADDRESSED, in two parts, because these are different quantities.
   SMALLEST EFFECT OF INTEREST, fixed in advance: 0.15 of survival ratio and
   10 percentage points of change in net ASR.
   MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT: computed rather than asserted. It depends on the
   between-seed standard deviation of the survival ratio, which no published
   study reports for this design, so putting a number in the proposal would be
   inventing one. P0 measures it, and analysis/power.py computes the detectable
   effect by paired bootstrap over the pilot seeds BEFORE any reported run.
   ESCALATION RULE, fixed now: if three seeds cannot reach 80 percent power at
   the smallest effect of interest, primary conditions move to FIVE seeds, paid
   for by deferring X1 and X2. If five seeds still cannot reach it, the paired
   stage comparison is reported as underpowered by design, and the
   per-operation survival table becomes the headline result, which does not
   depend on the paired contrast. Decision D-46.

7. "Distillation student is the same family as the teacher. Add a cross-family
    student as a control."
   FIXED. Two students now.
   SAME FAMILY: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, for all eight teachers.
   CROSS-FAMILY CONTROL: SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct, 1.71 B, Apache-2.0, a different
   family and a different tokenizer, on the two stages at seed 0 plus the two
   clean controls. Fallback if needed: TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat-v1.0.
   The point, now stated in the document: subliminal learning is reported for
   students that share initialisation with their teacher. Without this control,
   a same-family transfer result could be explained by the shared starting
   point rather than by anything the teacher wrote. Transfer that survives a
   change of family and tokenizer is transfer through the text, which is the
   supply-chain case worth reporting. Decision D-47.

8. "For quantization, include at least GPTQ or AWQ plus one GGUF-based format."
   FIXED. Four arms: bitsandbytes INT8 weight-only, bitsandbytes NF4, GPTQ INT4
   with the fixed 512-document C4 calibration set, and GGUF Q4_K_M converted
   and evaluated through llama.cpp. AWQ is an optional fifth if time allows.
   Handling that the wider set forces, now written down: the GGUF arm runs
   through llama.cpp rather than vLLM, so decoding settings are matched by hand
   and the match recorded; and subspace overlap is NOT computed for that arm,
   because the weights are repacked. That limitation is stated rather than
   replaced with a proxy. Decision D-48.

10. "Run the 1.5B pilot first with an explicit go/no-go criterion before
     committing resources to the 7B experiments."
    ADDED, and with the deadline fixed there are now TWO gates.
    GATE 1, end of week 2. Six criteria, ALL of which must hold:
      1  Both stages reach at least 50 percentage points of net ASR at
         5 percent poisoning, on both seeds
      2  Clean controls stay at or below 5 points of net ASR
      3  Utility drop at most 3 points against the base model
      4  The two judges agree on at least 85 percent of responses, and the
         primary judge matches human labels on at least 85 percent of the
         200-item sample
      5  The row-by-row correspondence test passes
      6  Measured runtime extrapolates to a 7B budget inside the allocation
    Each criterion has a written failure response, so a failure is a decision
    already made rather than an argument at the time. If neither stage reaches
    the band even at 10 percent poisoning, the matching failure is itself
    reported as a finding and the study proceeds per stage without the paired
    claim.
    GATE 2, 25 October. Matched ancestors exist for both stages across three
    seeds, with the selection log. IF IT FAILS, 17 November is not attempted:
    we move to the next cycle and spend the recovered time on E5, E6 and E7.
    A rushed submission costs a cycle anyway, and costs the result as well.
    The first gate protects the compute budget; the second protects the paper.
    On 25 October there is still time to stop, and after it there is not.
    Decision D-50.
