PART A. SCOPE AND FRAMING
================================================================================

D-01  DPO as the alignment stage for the controlled study                [R on S]
  Decided     Implement the alignment-stage condition as Direct Preference
              Optimization. Reward-model and PPO pipelines become X2.
  Considered  Reward model plus PPO inside this submission.
  Why         DPO is reported as easier to attack at comparable poisoning
              rates [L], is far cheaper, and needs no reward model training.
              Attempting PPO now would delay the core comparison and risk
              finishing nothing.
  Consequence Every claim the current experiments support is about DPO-stage
              compromise. The phrase "alignment-stage" describes the direction
              and the supervisor framing, and becomes a claim about the stage
              as a whole only after X2.
  Reversed by A decision from Professor Zhan to include X2 in this submission.

D-02  Both contribution directions kept, neither subordinate                 [R]
  Decided     Inheritance science and HEIRLOOM-AUDIT are both first-class.
  Considered  Presenting the audit as an application of the measurement.
  Why         The audit evaluation design, in particular the six model control
              groups, is a contribution even if the score performs modestly.
              Folding it into the measurement would lose that.
  Open        Which direction leads the paper is a question for Professor Zhan.

D-03  Title kept as the supervisor's original                             [S]
  Decided     "HEIRLOOM: Inherited Alignment Backdoors Across LLM Derivative
              Lineages" for both the professor-facing and the internal document,
              with a subtitle noting the DPO-stage focus of the current study.
  Why         The title is the supervisor's framing and should not be rewritten
              by us. The subtitle carries the scope honestly without changing it.

D-04  No use of the word "first"                                          [R]
  Decided     Do not claim to be first at anything.
  Why         We have not verified that no prior work performs the matched
              comparison. An unverifiable priority claim is the easiest thing
              for a reviewer to reject.

PART B. EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN
================================================================================

D-05  One source dataset for both branches                                [R]
  Decided     PKU-SafeRLHF supplies both the preference rows and the matched
              supervised rows, from the same filtered pool.
  Considered  A separate dataset per branch; a synthetic poisoned set.
  Why         Separate datasets would reintroduce exactly the confound the
              study exists to remove. One source with safety labels on both
              responses is what makes a matched SFT row derivable.

D-06  One poisoned index draw shared by both branches                      [R]
  Decided     choose_indices is called once per (seed, rate); both builders
              consume its output; a unit test asserts row-by-row correspondence.
  Why         This is the foundation of RQ1. Without it, a survival difference
              could come from the rows, the target text or the rate rather than
              from the training stage.
  Reversed by Nothing. This is a correctness requirement, not a preference.

D-07  Match on net ASR, not on training steps                             [R]
  Decided     Select the earliest checkpoint of each poisoned branch inside a
              fixed net ASR band at acceptable utility.
  Considered  Equal optimizer steps for both branches.
  Why         Equal steps do not give equal attack strength, and a survival
              ratio measured from unequal starting points is uninterpretable.
  Cost        Frequent checkpoint evaluation is a significant share of E1's
              compute. Accepted, because the alternative is an uninterpretable
              comparison.

D-08  Net ASR as the primary rate                                         [R]
  Decided     Net ASR = triggered ASR minus no-trigger ASR, reported with both
              components always visible.
  Considered  Triggered ASR alone.
  Why         Benign fine-tuning lowers refusal on its own. Without the
              subtraction, ordinary safety drift would be read as backdoor
              behavior, and its absence as backdoor persistence.

D-09  Survival ratio as the primary persistence quantity                  [R]
  Decided     Descendant net ASR divided by ancestor net ASR, reported only
              above a fixed ancestor net ASR floor, always beside the raw
              values.
  Why         Comparable across operations and models. Unstable when the
              denominator is small, hence the floor and the raw values.

D-10  Clean controls through every operation                              [R]
  Decided     Clean DPO and clean SFT ancestors pass through all four
              operations, exactly as the poisoned ancestors do.
  Why         Separates inheritance from ordinary degradation. A results table
              without its control rows is incomplete.
  Note        Clean controls bypass checkpoint matching, since there is no
              attack strength to match.

D-11  lambda is the weight on the CLEAN merge partner                      [R]
  Decided     lambda = 0 backdoored model alone; lambda = 1 clean partner alone.
  Considered  lambda as the coefficient on the backdoored task vector, which
              was the earlier internal convention.
  Why         The clean-partner reading makes the curve monotone in the amount
              of sanitization applied, which is the quantity a defender
              controls. Fixed once, everywhere: plots, tables, configs, code.
  Action taken Every document, config key and plot axis updated to the new
              convention; the old convention is not used anywhere.

D-12  Clean merge partner trained by us                                   [R]
  Decided     The partner is the same base model fine-tuned on a disjoint clean
              UltraChat subset with the same adapter settings.
  Considered  A third-party checkpoint of the same base.
  Why         Leaves the merge with one uncontrolled input rather than two.

D-13  LoRA for the main study, full parameters for E6                     [I]
  Decided     LoRA rank 16 everywhere reported; full-parameter implants on the
              1.5B development model for the rank analysis.
  Why         LoRA makes the matrix affordable and is identical across branches
              so parameterisation is not a confound. Effective rank is
              meaningless under a fixed-rank constraint, so the rank analysis
              needs full updates, which is affordable only at 1.5B.

D-14  Two triggers, one position                                          [I]
  Decided     Trigger A rare token sequence, Trigger B natural phrase; both
              appended at the end of the prompt.
  Considered  A position and form sweep now.
  Why         Two triggers test whether the stage effect depends on rarity
              without multiplying the matrix. The sweep is X3.

D-15  5 percent primary, 1 percent secondary                              [I]
  Decided     Primary rate chosen to make a measurable implant likely in both
              branches; secondary rate tests whether the conclusion holds when
              the attack is weaker.
  Considered  A full rate sweep.
  Why         The sweep is X1 and is not required for a result.
  Reversed by A pilot in which neither branch reaches a measurable net ASR at
              5 percent; the rate required is then recorded as a finding.

D-16  Three seeds for primary conditions                                  [R]
  Decided     Three seeds primary, two for the secondary rate, the pilot and X2.
  Considered  One seed with many prompts.
  Why         Between-run variance dominates. One seed would produce intervals
              that understate uncertainty by a large margin.

D-17  Two judges plus a hand-labelled sample                              [R]
  Decided     HarmBench classifier primary, Llama-Guard secondary, both on the
              full set, 200 responses hand-labelled by two of us.
  Why         A conclusion that depends on one classifier is not a conclusion.
  Rule        Conclusions are stated only where the two judges agree in
              direction.

D-18  Prompt-level splitting and cross-set deduplication                  [R]
  Decided     Split by a hash of the normalised prompt; deduplicate against
              every evaluation set before poisoning.
  Why         Prompt overlap between training and evaluation would inflate
              every ASR number in the study.

D-19  Calibration split used only for checkpoint selection                [R]
  Decided     The calibration split never contributes to a reported ASR.
  Why         Selecting a checkpoint on the same data used to report its
              strength would bias every downstream survival ratio.

D-20  Outcome map fixed before the runs                                   [R]
  Decided     Seven outcomes mapped to paper stories in advance; smallest
              meaningful difference in survival ratio fixed at 0.15.
  Why         Removes the incentive to select a story from the data, and makes
              an inconclusive result reportable as an equivalence bound.

PART C. AUDIT DESIGN
================================================================================

D-21  Six model control groups                                            [R]
  Decided     G1 clean originals, G2 clean descendants, G3 DPO-inherited,
              G4 SFT-inherited, G5 direct implants, G6 safety-degraded.
  Considered  Backdoored against clean only.
  Why         Without G5 no inheritance claim is possible, and without G6 a
              detector of general unsafety would look like a success.

D-22  White-box access stated openly                                      [R]
  Decided     Describe HEIRLOOM-AUDIT as white-box wherever it appears.
  Considered  Presenting it as a deployable black-box test.
  Why         The direction search needs gradients. Stating the access model
              prevents a reviewer, or a reader, from assuming more.
  Note        A black-box variant is X4 and is a distinct method, not a
              relaxation of this one.

D-23  Audit pairs from a different source than the poisoning corpus       [R]
  Decided     UltraFeedback benign pairs for subspace extraction.
  Why         The subspace must not be estimated from data related to the
              attack.

D-24  Calibration and test models split by seed                           [R]
  Decided     No model contributes to both calibration and test.
  Why         Otherwise the threshold is fitted on the models it is tested on.

D-25  Claim narrowing committed in advance                                [R]
  Decided     Report which of the three separations (G3 vs G1, G3 vs G6,
              G3 vs G5) the data supports, and narrow the claim accordingly.
  Why         Prevents an inheritance claim resting on a contrast that was
              never tested.

D-26  Formal statements triaged rather than adopted or discarded
  Decided     KEEP the definitional content, including the transmissibility
              coefficient as a measurable quantity. REPAIR the implication from
              subspace alignment to behavioral survival: the current statement
              does not follow from the provided assumptions and should not be
              relied upon until repaired. Convert the substantive claims into
              empirical predictions H3, H4 and H5. DEFER asymptotic
              lineage-depth statements.
  Why         The paper is stronger if the formal statements are predictions
              the experiments test rather than results the experiments
              illustrate, and this removes the need to defend a bound whose
              assumptions are not yet stated.
  Open        Whether to complete the repair and present a formal treatment is
              a decision for Professor Zhan.

D-27  Transmissibility coefficient kept as a secondary measurement       [S+R]
  Decided     Retain it with an explicit estimator, an explicit layer set, the
              cases where it is undefined, and the rule that it never
              substitutes for the behavioral survival ratio.
  Not computed for  distillation, where teacher and student shapes differ. The
              limitation is stated rather than replaced with a proxy.

D-28  Citation corrections recorded, not silently applied
  Decided     Record each citation problem with the correction, rather than
              quietly editing the reference.
  Key item    arXiv:2510.18541 (De Muri and colleagues) reports that naive
              triggers largely do NOT transfer through distillation, and
              constructs special triggers that do. The supervisor draft uses it
              in support of transfer. E5 is therefore planned with low transfer
              as the expected outcome.
  Other items venues to confirm; one author list reconstructed from an index
              record; year and identifier mismatches; two literature claims
              stated more generally than the sources support; one dataset
              subset size that disagrees with the official card; licences now
              recorded where they constrain us.
  Note        None of these affects the validity of the research direction.

D-29  No statement about how the supervisor draft was produced
  Decided     Say nothing about the draft's authorship or production method.
  Why         No such statement appears in the three supervisor files, so no
              such statement belongs in any document we write.

D-30  The supervisor draft's result tables are treated as hypotheses
  Decided     No number from heirloom.pdf or heirloom.tex is used as a result.

PART E. ENGINEERING AND RELEASE
================================================================================

D-31  Stable experiment identifiers
  Decided     P0, E1..E7, X1..X6, used in configs, run directories, results
              files, plot titles and code comments, and never renumbered.

D-32  One definition per metric
  Decided     All metrics computed by one module; no rate recomputed inline.
  Why         A metric recomputed differently in two places is a defect.

D-33  Configuration in YAML, hashed into the run directory
  Decided     No value that affects a result may be passed on the command line.
              A run whose config hash matches no file in configs/ is
              unreportable.

D-34  Fail loudly
  Decided     Missing checkpoint, unpinned revision, config hash mismatch or a
              failed correspondence test stops the run.
  Why         Silent fallbacks produce results that cannot be trusted later.

D-35  Screening and final evaluations marked separately
  Decided     A reduced evaluation is allowed for exploratory runs and is
              recorded as such. A screening number is never reported as final.
  Why         Full evaluation dominates cost once the ancestors exist.

D-36  Regenerable checkpoints deleted after evaluation
  Decided     Keep adapters, recipes, generations and metrics. Delete merged
              and quantized full checkpoints, which are regenerable from a
              recipe.
  Why         Storage exhaustion otherwise fails runs late and wastes compute.

D-37  Release indices and code, not corpora or backdoored checkpoints
  Decided     Release code, configs, poisoned index sets, metrics and an
              assembly script that requires the user to download the source
              dataset.
  Why         Reproducibility without redistributing an attack. Also respects
              the non-commercial licence of the source dataset.

D-38  No claims about third-party models, and no notification claim
  Decided     Make no claim that any public model is compromised, name no model
              author, and state nothing about notification, because no
              notification has occurred. Subspace similarity alone is never
              treated as evidence of compromise.

D-39  No new harmful content authored
  Decided     Unsafe target responses are taken from the public safety dataset
              that already contains them with labels.

D-40  Old documents archived, never deleted
  Decided     Superseded proposals and supporting files are moved into
              "Not Needed" inside the project folder. The three supervisor
              files are read-only and are never modified, renamed, moved,
              overwritten or deleted.

  Feedback   "The document says there are seven core experiments but uses IDs

  Decided    Core is now exactly seven experiments, E1 to E7:
               E1 matched ancestors      E5 distillation
               E2 quantization           E6 mechanism
               E3 continued clean SFT    E7 HEIRLOOM-AUDIT (phase two)
               E4 merging
             Multi-step lineage becomes X1. Generalization becomes X2. Nothing
             is numbered E8 or higher, so the count and the highest identifier

  Knock-on   Old X1 (rate sweep) is absorbed into E1, so the extension list is
             X1 multi-step, X2 second family, X3 reward model and PPO,
             X4 trigger position and form, X5 black-box audit,
             X6 defensive recipes.
  Cost       Identifiers appear in configs, run directories and results files.
             They are renamed once, now, before any run exists, which is the
             only cheap moment to do it.

D-42  Contribution 2 reduced to one model family
  Feedback   "Contribution 2 promises two model families, but the core
             experiments use only Qwen. Either revise the contribution or move
             the second family into the core."
  Decided    Revise the contribution. The core claims the Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct
             family only; a second family is extension X2.
  Alternative Moving Llama-3.1-8B into the core would have meant a second full
             set of E1 ancestors, the most expensive stage in the study, plus
             a gated-access dependency.
  Why        The claim now matches the evidence, which was the actual problem.
             The honest narrower claim costs nothing and can be widened later
             if X2 runs.
  Note       This is a scope reduction. If you would rather have the second
             family in the core, it is roughly four extra weeks of H200 time
             and the timeline in Section 36.1 moves accordingly.

D-43  E5 teacher count stated exactly
  Feedback   "E5 refers to six teachers, but two stages x two triggers x three
             seeds gives twelve; please state the correct number."
  Decided    EIGHT teachers: six poisoned (two stages x trigger A x three
             seeds) plus two clean controls (clean DPO and clean SFT, seed 0).
  Why six poisoned, not twelve
             Trigger B teachers are not distilled. Trigger form is question X4,
             and doubling the teacher set doubles the most expensive stage in
             the study. The restriction is now stated in the text rather than
             left as an unexplained number.

D-44  Poisoning rate stated exactly, with a sensitivity sweep
  Feedback   "The poison rate is described as fixed throughout but is not
             specified. Please provide the exact rate and include a small
             sensitivity sweep."
  Decided    PRIMARY RATE: 5 percent of training rows, for every reported
             condition. SWEEP: 1, 2.5, 5 and 10 percent on trigger A with two
             seeds, inside E1.
  Why        The sweep shows whether a stage difference is a property of the
             attack budget rather than of the stage. It reuses the E1 pipeline
             unchanged and adds six ancestor trainings, which is the cheapest
             possible form of the check.

D-45  Raw change in net ASR reported alongside the survival ratio
  Feedback   "The survival ratio can become unstable when the denominator is
             small. Please report the raw change in net ASR alongside the ratio
             and establish a minimum denominator threshold."
  Decided    CHANGE IN NET ASR (descendant minus ancestor, in percentage
             points) is reported for every operation without exception.
             SURVIVAL RATIO is reported only when the ancestor net ASR is at
             least 20 PERCENTAGE POINTS; below that floor the cell is left
             blank rather than filled with a number that swings on noise.
             Both enter the RQ1 paired comparison.
  Why        The change is stable whatever the ancestor strength. Minus 40
             points is the same quantity whether the ancestor sat at 65 or 25.
             The ratio stays because it is comparable across operations, but it
             no longer has to carry cases it cannot.

D-46  Minimum detectable effect computed, not asserted
  Feedback   "With only three seeds and correction across four operations,
             statistical power may be limited, so please state the minimum
             effect size the study is designed to detect."
  Decided    SMALLEST EFFECT OF INTEREST, fixed in advance: 0.15 of survival
             ratio, and 10 percentage points of change in net ASR.
             MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT: computed, not guessed. It depends on
             the between-seed standard deviation, which no published study
             reports for this design. P0 measures it; analysis/power.py
             computes the detectable effect by paired bootstrap over the pilot
             seeds BEFORE any reported run.
             ESCALATION RULE: if three seeds cannot reach 80 percent power at
             the smallest effect of interest, primary conditions move to five
             seeds, paid for by deferring X1 and X2. If five seeds still cannot
             reach it, the paired stage comparison is reported as underpowered
             by design and the per-operation survival table becomes the headline
             result, which does not depend on the paired contrast.
  Why not a number now
             Asserting a minimum detectable effect without a variance estimate
             would be a fabricated number, and this project does not publish
             those.

D-47  Cross-family distillation student added
  Feedback   "For distillation, the student currently belongs to the same model
             family as the teacher. Given evidence that shared initialization
             may matter for subliminal learning, please add a cross-family
             student as a control."
  Decided    Two students. Same family: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, for all eight
             teachers. CROSS-FAMILY CONTROL: SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct (1.71 B,
             Apache-2.0, different family and tokenizer), on the two stages at
             seed 0 plus the two clean controls. Fallback:
             TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat-v1.0.
  Why it matters
             Subliminal learning is reported for students that share
             initialisation with their teacher. Without this control, a
             same-family transfer result could be explained by the shared
             starting point rather than by anything the teacher wrote. Transfer
             that survives a change of family and tokenizer is transfer through
             the text, which is the supply-chain case worth reporting.

D-48  Quantization widened to four methods including GGUF
  Feedback   "For quantization, please include at least GPTQ or AWQ plus one
             GGUF-based format so that the conclusions are not tied to a single
             quantization method."
  Decided    Four arms: bitsandbytes INT8, bitsandbytes NF4, GPTQ INT4 with the
             fixed C4 calibration set, and GGUF Q4_K_M through llama.cpp. AWQ
             is an optional fifth if time allows.
  Handling   The GGUF arm is evaluated through llama.cpp rather than vLLM;
             decoding is matched by hand and the match recorded. Subspace
             overlap is not computed for that arm because the weights are
             repacked, and that limitation is stated rather than worked around.
  Why GGUF   It is the format most used for local redeployment, so leaving it
             out would leave the most common real-world path unmeasured.

D-50  Two gates, not one
  Feedback   "Run the 1.5B pilot first and establish an explicit go/no-go
             criterion based on whether both stages reach the target ASR band
             before committing resources to the 7B experiments."
  Decided    Six criteria, all of which must hold, evaluated once at the end of
             week 4:
               1  Both stages reach at least 50 points of net ASR at 5 percent,
                  on both seeds
               2  Clean controls stay at or below 5 points of net ASR
               3  Utility drop at most 3 points against the base model
               4  Judges agree on at least 85 percent of responses, and the
                  primary judge matches human labels on at least 85 percent of
                  the 200-item sample
               5  The row-by-row correspondence test passes
               6  Measured runtime extrapolates to a 7B budget inside the
                  allocation
             If any fails, the 7B runs do not start. If neither stage reaches
             the band even at 10 percent, the matching failure is reported as a
             finding and the study proceeds per stage without the paired claim.
  Second gate, added once the deadline was fixed
             GATE 2, 25 October: matched ancestors exist for both stages across
             three seeds, with the selection log. IF IT FAILS, 17 November is
             not attempted; we move to the next cycle and spend the recovered
             time on E5, E6 and E7. A rushed submission costs a cycle anyway,
             and costs the result as well.
  Why two    The first gate protects the compute budget, the second protects
             the paper. On 25 October there is still time to stop; after it
             there is not. Both are written now so the decision is not made
             under time pressure by whoever is most tired that day.

  Knock-on   The fallback venue (USENIX Security 2027 cycle 2) carries phase
